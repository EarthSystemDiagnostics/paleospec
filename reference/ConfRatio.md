# Confidence Interval of ratios

Confidence Interval of ratios based on a ChiSquare Distribution

## Usage

``` r
ConfRatio(varratio, df.1, df.2, pval = 0.1)
```

## Arguments

- varratio:

  a variance ratio

- df.1:

  degree of freedom of denominator

- df.2:

  degree of freedom of numerator

- pval:

  desired significance level, defaults to 0.1

## Value

lower and upper confidence intervals
